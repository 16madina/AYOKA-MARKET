Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Published standards for AYOKA MARKET, Your Second Life Marketplace (Google Play store listing name).

Explicit prohibition of CSAE

AYOKA MARKET, Your Second Life Marketplace explicitly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including but not limited to:

  • Any sexual content involving a minor (real or fictional)
  • Grooming or sexual solicitation of a minor
  • Production, possession, sharing, or promotion of child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Trafficking or sexual exploitation of children

Any user who violates this policy will be permanently banned. Content will be removed. Where required by law, we will report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authorities (including NCMEC or the relevant regional authority).

Scope

These published standards apply to the mobile application AYOKA MARKET, Your Second Life Marketplace as displayed on Google Play, and to all users, listings, messages, and other user-generated content on the platform.

Age restriction

AYOKA MARKET, Your Second Life Marketplace is for users aged 18+ only. Minors are not permitted to create accounts or use the service.

Enforcement & reporting

  • In-app “Report” on listings and in messaging
  • Content moderation and automated filtering
  • User blocking
  • Priority handling of CSAE reports
  • Permanent ban for CSAE violations

Child Safety point of contact: lazoneclient@gmail.com (subject: “CSAE / Child Safety”). This contact can discuss enforcement procedures and take action.
